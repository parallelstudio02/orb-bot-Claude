# ORB Signal Bot

Opening Range Breakout trading bot for US market open (9:30 PM SGT).

Built for $1,000 capital, $30 max loss per trade, 1:2 risk/reward.

## What it does
- Shows 5 pre-market signals scored against 5 conditions
- Calculates exact share size, stop loss, and target for each trade
- Tracks session P&L, win rate, consecutive losses, and capital health
- Saves session history to your browser
- Warns you when to stop trading
